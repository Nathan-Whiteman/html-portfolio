My top 3 movies of all time

Zootopia

Story on a fox and bunny becoming partners despite being different.

Zootopia 2

Story about becoming a strong bond despite difference when tackling a devide on species of animals while trying to take over land.

Ready Player One

Story about virtual reality mixing in with real life. Earning money through virtual reality. However, also emphasises points such as online safety and keeping personal details private, the importance of spending time away from virtual realities and also that corporations will always try to cheat their way to the top.
