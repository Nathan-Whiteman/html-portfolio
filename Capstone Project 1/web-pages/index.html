Summary

I graduated from Nottingham Trent University in 2023 with a BA (Hons) in Illustration. Although I enjoyed my degree, I decided to pursue my longstanding interest in IT and software development, having recognised that this was the career path I wanted to follow. Since then, I have completed a Level 3 IT Technician Apprenticeship and gained practical experience at Passport365, providing Tier 1 helpdesk support for the company's software, alongside developing my skills in HTML, CSS and JavaScript through creating PDF forms. My role has also allowed me to develop strong communication and client relationship skills, regularly liaising with key clients, delivering software demonstrations and providing support both remotely via Microsoft Teams and in person. In addition, I have worked closely with internal developers, playing a key role in testing newly developed software, ensuring existing workflows were maintained and new features worked as intended. I am now looking to build on my experience, further develop my technical skills and establish a long-term career in IT and software development.
